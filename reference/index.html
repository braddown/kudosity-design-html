Content width

Max 1240px. Prose 760px. That’s the reading line; don’t stretch a paragraph to the app edge.

App chrome

248px sidebar. 64px sticky top bar. Those two are the only fixed app chrome.

Marketing chrome

74px glass header. Full-bleed navy footer. No floating chat bubbles. No sticky banners.

Elevation

Five steps, navy rgba(0,42,102,…). Hovered primary buttons pick up a violet-tinted brand shadow. Never a black drop shadow.

Motion

140ms hover, 220ms state, 360ms reveal. Standard easing. No bounce, spring, parallax or scroll-jacking. Rise 8px at most.

Hover, press, focus

Hover: one step darker, lift 2px. Press: scale(0.98). Focus: 3px violet halo at 38%. Disabled: 45% opacity, never grey-swapped text.

Blur — three places only

Sticky header, glass cards over colour, dialog scrim. Never blur text. Never frost a whole page.

Product logo suite

Eight products, one mark family. Violet body, exactly one blush element. Horizontal lockup at 44px for app chrome. Marks alone at 20–32px. by Kudosity is grey-500 at 34% of the product name — required outward-facing.

Imagery

Real people, mid-laugh, warm wardrobes, soft daylight. Never cool, black and white, grainy or stiff stock. Text over photos uses a gradient scrim, never a capsule.

Secondary scenes

Quieter workplace photographs for blog, developer and customer tiles. Primary imagery can take a brand shape. Secondary stays plain.

Patterns

Line-wave and arrow curves. Large, low-contrast, off-canvas — about 18% opacity in a hero. Not tiled. No grain.

Product graphics

Fragments of the real UI, glassy white over violet, thin magenta connector. Never an illustration of an interface.

Protection

Gradient scrims, not capsules. Bottom scrim for a caption on a photo; left scrim for text beside one.

If contrast fails

Move the text off the image. Don’t drop a solid box on the photo.

Iconography

Kudosity ships no icon set. The O device is a logo, never a toolbar icon. UI icons are Lucide, 2px rounded-cap — a flagged stand-in until an in-house set exists. Unicode only for … pagination and — empty cells. No emoji in Kudosity UI.

Buttons

Pills, always. Labels never take a full stop. Hovered primary picks up the brand shadow.

Voice

Funny, casual, everyday, irreverent, plain English. Never serious, formal, expert-y, stuffy, traditional or technical. A blush full stop on the H1, once per view — never in body copy, never on a button.

Active voice

Start your 14-day trial today. Not “a 14-day trial is available”.

Make the complex simple

If it takes a second sentence to explain the first, rewrite the first.

Be informative, then stop

Say the thing. Don’t dump the wiki.

Speak conversationally

Contractions are welcome. And and But can start a sentence. Say us, not Kudosity; you, not customers.

Don’t intimidate

We’re on your side. Never talk down, never talk over.

Don’t be bossy

Invite, don’t order. The product does the heavy lifting.

Benefits over features

What they get, not what the stack does.

Don’t overdo wordplay

A wink is enough. If you hear yourself being clever, stop.

Person, emoji, casing

Person

We and you. Never “Kudosity enables organisations to…”.

Emoji

Not part of the brand system. Say emoticon in copy. An emoticon may appear inside a customer message (the composer picker) — never in our own UI labels, headings, marketing copy or slides.

Casing

Sentence case everywhere. Tiny uppercase micro-labels — eyebrows, table headers — are the one exception. Brand full stop is blush, once per view, on the H1 only.

Microcopy

Numbers take thousands separators; rates one decimal. Australian English — personalise, organisation.

Labels

Sentence case, no colon. The field already says what it is.

Hints

Explain, don’t instruct. A hint is a why, not a how.

Errors

Describe what happened. Then how to get moving again.

Empty and success

Stay warm. The product just did something — or nothing yet. Don’t go cold.
