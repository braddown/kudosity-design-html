Campaign brief

Artwork comes from the Kudosity design system and a campaign brief — not from scraping the marketing website, and not from a single image prompt.

Source and input

Logos, colours, type and voice from the design system. The brief names funnels, offer, CTA and audience.

Output

The full unit set for the platforms in the brief — PMAX, GDN, Reddit, Meta, LinkedIn as named. One artwork family per funnel. Three funnels → three families × the sizes below.

Build the four masters first. Crop PMAX, Reddit, Meta and the LinkedIn carousel. Relayout GDN. Crop LinkedIn portrait from the vertical master — not from the 4:5.

Margins and safe areas

Starting points. Testing is still recommended.

Minimum margin

At least 50px on all sides of a 1080px canvas, and the same between elements. Scale proportionally on smaller canvases.

Stories / Reels

160–200px clear top and bottom. Profile row and CTA sit in that band.

Logo clear space

O-height of clear space on top of the canvas margin. Digital minimum width 140px. On GDN sizes under 200px wide, use the O device instead of the wordmark.

Text over imagery

Gradient scrim, never a solid capsule. If contrast fails, move the text off the image.

Type floor

Nothing below 24px on a 1080 canvas. GDN banners under 100px tall: headline only, drop body copy.

Export

PNG for flat brand colour or UI screenshots. JPG at 85% for photography. Keep assets under 1MB.
